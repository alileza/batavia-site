The Studio

Particular about our machines, since 2010.

Born from the love of premium denim, Batavia Jeans Studio was established in 2010 to keep every pair — old or new — fitting perfectly and lasting longer. Each of our dozen specialized machines is dedicated to a single stitch, so every job is done quickly with quality comparable to the original, and covered by a lifetime guarantee.

Indigo-Dyed Repair Thread

For repairs we use a special thread imported from the USA — indigo-dyed, cotton-wrapped, polyester-core. The feel and look stay as close as possible to the denim itself, and because the cotton wrap is indigo-dyed, the repair fades along with your jeans.
