the studio

Since 2010

The Studio

Born from the love of premium-quality jeans, Batavia Jeans Studio was established in 2010 to provide the best, most reliable denim service — making sure every pair, old or new, always fits perfectly, lives longer, and keeps up with your evolving style.

Each machine on our floor is specialized for a different stitch, so every job is done quickly with stitching comparable to the original — and covered by a lifetime guarantee.

Union Special 35800DN

The crown of our vintage heavy-machinery collection: a feed-off-the-arm, 3-needle chainstitch machine, made in USA. The 35800 series was introduced in the late '60s and ran in the top American factories of the era — the facilities that produced Levi's, Lee and Wrangler — and it still runs in the world's best manufacturers today.

It produces a beautiful felled seam with double or triple-needle chainstitch, and its feed-off-the-arm shape lets us fell both inseam and outseam when needed.

In Batavia Jeans Studio, we are particular about our machines.

Indigo-Dyed Repair Thread

For repairs we use a special thread imported from the USA: indigo-dyed, cotton-wrapped polyester-core. The feel and look stay as close as possible to the actual denim — and because the cotton wrap is indigo-dyed, the repair fades along with your jeans.
